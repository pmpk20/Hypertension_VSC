# Data licence

The survey responses, page timings, codebook, reference statistics and questionnaire illustrations in this folder are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This includes Figures 1 and 2 in `Reference_Figures/`.

You may share and adapt the data for any purpose, provided you give appropriate credit, link to the licence and indicate whether changes were made.

When reusing the data, please cite:

- the OECD SWACHE project, for which the survey was designed by the OECD Environment Directorate and fielded online by Ipsos;
- the paper and this archive (Zenodo DOI to be added).

`Data/Other/` holds OECD reference statistics (purchasing power parities, GDP per capita and out-of-pocket health expenditure); cite the OECD as their source.
